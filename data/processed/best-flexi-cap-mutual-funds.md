## Overview

Flexi-cap mutual funds are open-ended equity funds that do not limit their investment to companies of a predetermined market cap. There are several equity mutual funds that invest in companies of a specific market capitalisation, such as a large-cap, mid-cap, or small-cap.

However, a flexi-cap mutual fund can invest in companies irrespective of their market size. Fund managers of flexi-cap mutual funds have greater flexibility when it comes to making investment decisions. Further, it allows investors to enjoy a diverse portfolio comprising companies of varying market caps from numerous sectors.

Flexi-cap mutual fund managers make investment decisions after considering various factors such as market trends, growth opportunities, and volatility. For example, a fund manager may allocate higher funds towards mid and small-cap companies when the market is performing well. When the market faces a downturn, the fund manager may move the funds towards the more stable large-cap funds to mitigate risk and navigate the volatility.

The investment style of a flexi cap mutual fund can be aggressive or conservative. A flexi-cap mutual fund with an aggressive approach may allocate more funds to mid and small-cap funds, while more conservative funds may allocate higher funds to large-cap funds.

Flexi-cap mutual funds are equity mutual funds, and gains on them attract capital gains tax.

Units sold within one year attract short-term capital gains tax at 15% in addition to the applicable surcharge or cess.
